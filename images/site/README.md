# Site photos

Save your own photos in this folder with the exact file names below. The site
picks them up automatically; no code changes are needed. Until a file is here,
that spot shows the shop's green gradient instead, and the repair banner is
left out.

To use other file names or folders, edit `$SITE_IMAGES` in
`includes/shop-config.php`.

| File | Where it appears | Size (at least) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero.jpg` | The banner at the top of the home page, and the blurred backdrop behind it | 2400 × 1400, landscape |
| `story.jpg` | The wide photo halfway down the home page, with text over its bottom-left | 2400 × 1100, landscape |
| `login.jpg` | The left half of the Log in page | 1200 × 1500, portrait |
| `signup.jpg` | The left half of the Sign up page | 1200 × 1500, portrait |
| `repair.jpg` | A banner at the top of the Repairs page | 2400 × 800, wide |

`favicon.svg` and `logo-mark.png` in this folder are the shop's icons, not photos.

## What to shoot

Deep greens, warm wood and soft daylight blend best with the design. Avoid
busy backgrounds and other shops' logos.

- **hero.jpg**: a laptop, perhaps with a phone, on a wooden desk by a window,
  in soft light. Keep the **left half calm and fairly dark**, because the
  headline sits there, and put the products on the right.
- **story.jpg**: someone working, studying or gaming at home or in a café.
  Keep the **bottom-left** simple for the text.
- **login.jpg**: a close-up of hands on a keyboard or holding a phone.
- **signup.jpg**: a new device being unboxed, or a parcel arriving.
- **repair.jpg**: a technician (a *fundi*) at a workbench repairing a phone or
  laptop, with tools in view.

Your own photos of the shop and the team work best. For stock photos, use a
site whose licence allows commercial use, such as Unsplash or Pexels.

## Before saving

1. Crop to the shape above, at that size or larger.
2. Save as JPG at about 80% quality, aiming for under 400 KB each.
3. Use the lowercase file names exactly as listed.
